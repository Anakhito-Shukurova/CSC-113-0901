# Reflection

## Biggest aha moment
My biggest “aha moment” was realizing I could create a fully working birthday invitation using AI without having any prior coding knowledge. It was surprising to see how something that would normally require technical skills could be built by clearly describing what I wanted. It changed how I view the relationship between creativity and technical ability, because I was able to focus more on the idea while the AI handled the execution.

## Time AI gave useless output
In the “bad bot” assignment, I asked the AI to maintain a specific role and stay fully in character. However, it eventually broke character once. The issue was easy to fix—I simply reinforced the instruction more clearly and directly told it not to repeat the mistake. After that adjustment, it followed the role consistently without further issues.

## How prompting changed
Over time, I became much more intentional and structured with my prompts. At first, my instructions were more general and less detailed, which sometimes led to inconsistent results. Now, I focus on being very clear, breaking down tasks step-by-step, and setting boundaries or expectations upfront. This significantly improved the quality and reliability of the AI’s responses.
