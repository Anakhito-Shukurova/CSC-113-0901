# My AI Project

## What is this?
One of the projects I worked on was a virtual themed children’s birthday invitation. The idea was to design a digital invitation that feels fun, interactive, and personalized, while still being easy for anyone to use. It was meant to mimic a real invitation card but in a customizable online format.

## What does it do?
The invitation includes blank fields that a user can fill in with their own event details, such as name, date, time, and location. Once completed, the user can save the finished invitation and send it digitally to the people they want to invite. The goal was to make the process of creating and sharing a themed invitation simple, fast, and visually engaging.

## How far did you get?
I fully completed the birthday card invitation. It functions as intended, and the final result works smoothly. The project feels finished and achieves the original idea of creating a usable and visually appealing digital invitation.

## What AI tools did you use?
I used ChatGPT and Claude on the same project in order to compare how each AI would perform when given the same task. This helped me understand the differences in output quality, structure, and problem-solving approaches between the tools.
